# Supplied literature archive and LANS comparison

Archived and reviewed 2026-10-28 (Asia/Shanghai).

**[Read the comparison and candidate research gap](comparison.md).** This compares the original full-command PPO-primary / independently executing LQR-backup proposal with the supplied corpus. It does not present historical residual experiments as validation of that proposal.

20 original PDF files, 294 pages including duplicate/version pages, 99,714,199 bytes: **19 unique files representing 18 works**. P09 and P20 are byte-identical; P04 and P08 are preprint and publisher versions of the same work. Original names and bytes are preserved. [Manifest](manifest.json) records sizes, page counts and SHA-256 hashes.

Review depth: targeted reading of methods, experiments and limitations, with closer examination of switching, timing and recovery evidence; previously verified reviews are reused for five works. This is not an independent experiment reproduction, audit of every equation/figure, or universal novelty search. All documents were text-extracted successfully. The inaccessible earlier ZIP is not part of this review.

Key findings: Simplex-Drive already has advanced/backup authority and recoverability-based return; ECRTS 2021 already analyzes deadline-miss bursts and recovery; the platooning paper already reports a multi-rate safety loop and ECU timing. No one common defect of all studies has been established. The report proposes a narrower, explicitly unverified joint recoverability/actuator/backup-response research question.
