The largest observed restrictions are low admitted authority, short valid packet lifetime relative to smoothing/recovery, model mismatch and limited exploration. Counts and probabilities in `diagnosis.json` are descriptive. The paired fresh experiment in `README.md` is needed to assess whether the numerical fix or exploration hypothesis helps; these findings alone do not prove a unique cause or that20% is impossible.
