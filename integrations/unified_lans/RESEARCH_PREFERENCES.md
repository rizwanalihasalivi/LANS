# Research execution preferences

User direction, 3 November 2026: complete the required Python experiments first;
target strong, outstanding results, then transfer the same frozen controller to
Webots for fresh validation only after Python promotion criteria pass.

Strong results must mean reproducible gains under matched comparisons, intact
safety/actuator/freshness checks and acceptable progress, smoothness and timing.
Retain all outcomes, including failed targets and regressions. Do not fabricate,
omit unfavorable cases, relax frozen thresholds after evaluation, retune on
reserved data or keep training until a preferred percentage appears. Diagnose
weaknesses on development data and test a separately declared improvement.

Webots supplies an independent transfer test; Python numbers must not be copied
into a Webots result table. No paid services or infrastructure are authorized.
