2. Published-source values: Standards ya technical literature se supported path geometry aur relevant physical constraints.
3. Simulation assumptions: Steering limits, actuator lag, sensor noise aur PPO training budget ki woh values jinhein verified vehicle data ya source abhi support nahi karta.

Kisi simulation assumption ko Tesla Model 3 ki verified specification nahi kahenge. Aur nominal value select karne ke baad sensitivity tests se check karenge ke controller ka performance us value par kitna dependent hai.

### Speed feasibility

25 m/s \\(=90\\) km/h ko aapka nominal speed baseline rehne dein, lekin severe double-lane-change course par ise automatically feasible assume na karein.

Har path ke liye:

\\[ a_y\approx v_x^2|\kappa| \\]

calculate karein, jahan \\(\kappa\\) path curvature hai. Initial screening ke liye \\(|a_y|\leq0.8\mu g\\) ka margin use kar sakte hain; yeh hamari proposed engineering assumption hogi, ISO requirement nahi.

- Smooth primary path 25 m/s par feasible ho toh use nominal test banayein.
- ISO-derived double-lane-change ko pehle 22.2 m/s ke aas-paas test karein.
- 25 m/s par course tabhi test karein jab curvature, friction aur nonlinear plant feasibility check pass ho.

Is tarah physically infeasible path ko controller ki failure ya success ke saath mix nahi kiya jayega.

Total training budget

## 1,000,000 environment steps per seed

Yeh proposed budget hai, original manuscript ka fully specified total nahi.

### Training curriculum

1. 200,000 steps: 15 m/s, latency injection ke baghair.
2. 400,000 steps: 25 m/s, latency injection ke baghair, feasible reference paths par.
3. 200,000 steps: 25 m/s, latency injection 10% probability ke saath; injected delay uniformly 5–130 ms. Yeh original manuscript ka training setup hai.
4. 200,000 steps: 15, 20 aur 25 m/s ke mixed feasible scenarios, latency disturbances ke saath final fine-tuning.

Final 200,000-step mixed phase hamari proposed addition hai. Asynchronous PPO mein injected delay ko actual output-availability delay ke taur par simulate karein, sirf numerical feature ke taur par nahi.

Meri additional recommendation:

- Har PPO-based policy ko 5 independent random seeds ke saath train karein.
- Har policy variant ko same environment-step budget dein.
- Validation results se convergence check karein.
- Final held-out test paths, latency traces aur random seeds ko training/tuning ke liye use na karein.
- PPO weights aur Supervisor thresholds held-out evaluation se pehle freeze kar dein.

Agar 1M steps par convergence establish nahi hoti, toh budget ko sab PPO variants ke liye equally increase karein aur change document karein.
