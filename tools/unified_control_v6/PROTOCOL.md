# Declared bounded proposal-completion experiment

This study begins after inspection of v5 development, not its unopened reserved
cases. Historical v2--v5 results and 5%/1% promotion decisions remain unchanged.
The user requests completion of the opening-report research and strong measured
results. An effect size is a hypothesis, not a promised outcome.

## One finite planning experiment

V5 perturbs four temporal blocks. It cannot independently optimize every future
actuator knot and discards its previously solved future plan. V6 projects a
shifted previous solution and performs three fixed per-knot sweeps of 0.01,
0.0025 and 0.000625 rad. Exact bicycle integration, braking, angle/rate/residual
limits, robust admission, failure criteria and all statistical gates stay fixed.

Compare exactly three candidates on the existing 64 training-only calibration
cases, never on reserved cases: v5 at 1 s, v6 at 1 s, v6 at 2 s. Evaluate both
nominal optimized and adaptive-only modes. All comparators share a selected
planning method/horizon; no model/seed winner is selected. Select zero failures,
at least 99% of maximum stable progress, heading/rate means no more than 10%
above shared LQR, then minimum original balanced calibration objective. Retain
every result. No iterative enlargement of this search follows its outcomes.

## Attribution and proposal evidence

After that finite experiment, freeze one version and a new development cohort.
Keep LQR/fixed/deterministic/nominal optimized/adaptive-only/PPO/hybrid references,
all three fixed PPO endpoints, and unchanged original bootstrap/statistical gates.
Component studies of prediction, freshness, risk supervision and adaptation
must label disabled protections as simulated diagnostic ablations, never deployment
candidates. Report physical motion, tracking, progress, collision, TTC/separation,
warning proxy labels, information ages, fallback and actual runtime separately.
Six-second evidence cannot establish long-run safety. Additional independent
traffic replicates and longer episodes must be explicitly labeled.

Untouched reserved, formal runtime promotion and final Webots remain conditional
on the existing promotion prerequisites. Failed hypotheses remain reportable
research findings; no false pass, outcome deletion or threshold relaxation is
allowed. Native or hardware results must never be inferred from Python metrics.
